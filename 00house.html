Frequently asked
What is the 00.house Tenant Pass?
+
The Tenant Pass is a verified digital identity card for renters. Apply once with your documents, credit history and references — then use it with any agent or landlord on the 00.house network worldwide. No need to repeat referencing for each property.
How does the keyholder viewing system work?
+
Property owners or agents register keyholders — trusted local individuals who can facilitate viewings on demand. Prospective tenants or buyers book a viewing slot through the platform. Keyholders are paid per completed viewing.
Can foreign tenants use the guarantor service?
+
Yes. Our guarantor partners cover international tenants who may not have a local credit history. We work with specialist insurers in the UK, UAE, France, Germany and Singapore who provide rent default protection accepted by landlords.
How do agents list properties on 00.house?
+
Agents register a free account and their listings are pulled automatically via our scraping engine from their existing portals. No manual data entry required. Premium placements and co-broking splits are managed inside the agent dashboard.
Which government portals does the legal hub link to?
+
We link directly to official government sources: UK GOV (Renters' Rights Act, Right to Rent checks), UAE RERA, Saudi Arabia Ministry of Housing / Ejar, French ANIL, Singapore HDB & URA, and US HUD. All links are verified and updated monthly.
Is 00.house available in my country?
+
We currently cover the UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, France, Germany, Singapore, Japan and the United States with dedicated legal frameworks. Our scraping network aggregates listings from 40+ countries. New markets are added monthly — search your city to check availability.

Questions agents ask

What will 00.house do with my listings?

Display your properties, photos, descriptions, prices, availability and viewing slots on 00.house, and promote them. We collect them regularly from the website or feed you give us, so they stay up to date.

What does it cost?

Registering is free, and agents who join before 20 November 2026 get 3 months of premium promotion. If a tenancy completes with a tenant introduced through 00.house, a success commission of 1% to 5% applies on completion, as set out in the Agent Terms.

Do you handle rent or deposits?

No. 00.house is a listings portal. It does not collect or hold rent, deposits or tenant payments. Every viewing and tenancy is managed directly by you.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. Contact 00.house and your listings will be removed.
